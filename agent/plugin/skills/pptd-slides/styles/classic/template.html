Why one log

Why we record events instead of updating rows

Updating rows in place makes every write wait for the one before it. At peak, more than half of our delay is spent waiting for locks.

An append-only log removes the wait: each event is a new row, keyed by account and sequence. Balances become a view we can rebuild, replay or audit at any time.

The cost is that reads may lag by up to a quarter of a second. No customer-facing flow depends on reading its own write, so nobody notices.
